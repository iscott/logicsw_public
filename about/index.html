About

Built for companies that
need things to work.

Logic Consulting was founded on a simple frustration: too much consulting, not enough doing. Every company we talked to had been through the same experience — pay a firm a lot of money, get a deck, get a roadmap, and then be left to figure out execution on their own.

We do it differently. We embed as operators, not advisors. When we take on a technical engagement, we are accountable for the outcome — not the hours.

Our focus is the Southwest United States, with particular depth in power generation, utilities, and professional services. We understand the specific constraints — regulatory, operational, and organizational — that make software in these sectors different from a typical web startup.

04 FAQ

Common questions

From technical buyers, operators, and first-time fractional CPTO clients.

What does "fractional CPTO" actually mean in practice?

It means you get a senior technical executive (Chief Product and Technology Officer) who is embedded in your company — attending your leadership meetings, managing your engineering team, making real architecture decisions — but structured as a contract engagement rather than a full-time hire. Typically 20–40 hours per month. You get executive-level judgment without the $300k+ fully-loaded cost.

How is this different from a typical software agency?

Most agencies optimize for scope and billable hours. We optimize for outcomes. We will tell you when you don't need something, recommend against a build if a SaaS tool covers 90% of the need, and we measure success by what ships and works — not by hours logged.

Do you work with companies that already have an engineering team?

Yes — and that's often our best engagement model. We work alongside your existing team as a technical leadership layer, a senior architect, or a specialist for a specific initiative your team doesn't have bandwidth for.

What does your AI implementation work involve?

It starts with a use-case discovery process where we identify where AI creates measurable ROI in your specific workflow. We prototype, validate, and then build production-grade systems. We don't hand you a prompt wrapper and call it an AI product — we build real systems with monitoring, accuracy tracking, and fallback logic.

What's your typical engagement timeline?

Discovery and scoping: 1–2 weeks. Architecture and planning: 2–4 weeks. Execution: project-dependent, but we establish clear milestones and deliverable gates from the start. Most initial engagements run 3–6 months, with many continuing as ongoing partnerships.

How do we get started?

Book a 30-minute discovery call. We'll ask about your business, your current technical state, and what you're trying to accomplish. If there's a fit, we'll scope a proposal within one week.
